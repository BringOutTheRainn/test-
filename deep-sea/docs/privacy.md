# Fathom privacy policy

*Last updated: October 16, 2026*

Fathom is a single-player idle game. This policy explains what information the game uses.

**What the game stores.** Your progress (Pearls, creatures, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not
have accounts, servers or analytics, and we never see your save.

**Ads.** Fathom offers optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. When you choose to watch one, Google
may collect and use your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), and
information about the ad and your device to show and measure ads. Google's use of this data is described at
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of
personalised ads, in your device's settings.

**Children.** Fathom is not directed at children under 13.

**Vibration.** The game asks for permission to vibrate for tap feedback. It uses no other device features.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

**Contact.** Questions: open an issue on the Fathom repository.
